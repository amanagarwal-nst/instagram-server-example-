Writes (like / comment / post) always travel to the database — and clear the cache on the way, because the data just changed.
Reads (refresh) check the cache first: a hit skips the database and comes back fast; a miss fetches from the database and refills the cache. The cache also expires on its own after a few seconds (its TTL).
